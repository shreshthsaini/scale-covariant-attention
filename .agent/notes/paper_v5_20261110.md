# Paper v5 (measurement-first rewrite), 2026-11-10 (paper owner)

User request via lead: the paper is complete but does not sell. Rewrite as a lean ICLR paper in five steps:
(1) observation (head explains 97.5% of the variance of near mass, noise level 2.5%; stable, transfers), (2) idea
(two native-size views, one number per head), (3) reach emulation (closed form, one table for all sizes), (4) use
(progressive SCA headline with margins, single-pass mode, fidelity trade-off in one sentence), (5) evidence that the
measurement matters (per-head beats shared and per-layer). Priority claims limited to: first variance decomposition of
reach across heads and steps; first per-head reach measured once at the training resolution and reused at any size
(UltraImage noted stable local/global head roles before us).
