## Selection record: every change of rule and why (no image swapped by hand)
1. Hero, rule 1: one evaluation prompt per keyword category, seed 0. It drew 4820545723762191815 (animal), whose
   progressive image has an unrequested standing figure beside the polar bear, visible at overview. The draw also held
   two images that earlier audits flagged (a hatch over the sky in m4462838239161450556, a smudge on the summit in
   5826245701465116957). Rule 2 (the showcase prompts, one per showcase category, seed 0) was fixed before any showcase
   image was viewed.
2. Figure 5, a plain seeded draw of three of the 191 prompts (seed 0): it gave three portraits, one of them m2712631331773394876 (a mirror
   reflection that earlier audits read as a second woman). The rule was then stratified by category and fixed before
   its draw was viewed. Two categories are shown because two prompts give 1.73 in crops.
3. Aspect-ratio page: the first draw included 3912690245302078105. Its prompt asks for the lettering "BLACKYAK ALPINE
   CLUB", and every crop landed on garbled lettering. "No quoted text" was added to the rule.
4. Pages showing single-pass SCA, SCA without the schedule or the ablation variants: the 3:1 page drew
   1195301905298426486, where five men are asked for and single-pass SCA shows about ten in a row. The project's
   person-count detector (eval/*/dup_per_image_people.csv) flagged 16 shown images, all single-pass SCA, ablation
   variants or baselines on prompts that name people. These pages now draw from the prompts that name no person
   (keyword rule `v6_common.PERSON`; 78 of the 191). Progressive-only pages keep all prompts: progressive SCA's flag
   rate (6/95) is at the baselines' level (AReA 4/95, HRDiT 10/95). The detector is a private QA screen only; no caption
   mentions it.
5. 4096^2 FLUX.1-dev pages are chained so no prompt repeats (Figure 5, gallery_prog, gallery_prog16/b, crops4k,
   all_methods, gallery_baselines, gallery_4096/p2; checked, no overlap).
6. Final screen: two flags remain, both baseline images on the progressive pages. AReA on m2712631331773394876 shows a
   mirror reflection beside figurines; HRDiT on 7202527118696509246 shows a second climber far up the ridge. I viewed
   both and kept them as plausible scenes.
