## Differences between the two harnesses (not the attention operator)
- Time shift: SCA uses Qwen's native mu 0.834 at every size. AReA's Qwen backbone sets mu 2.0 up to 4096^2 and
  2.0 + 0.5 ln(tokens / 256^2) above (2.26 at 5312^2). This is the SD3 resolution rule (e^mu proportional to the side):
  0.834 + ln(5312 / 1328) = 2.22, 0.834 + ln(4096 / 1328) = 1.96. At 40 steps, steps above sigma 0.858: 11 (SCA) vs
  23 (AReA at 5312^2); below 0.3: 6 vs 2.
- Conditioning: AReA appends QWEN_SUFFIX (", Ultra HD, 4K, cinematic composition, faithful to the described content,
  quantities, style and medium, coherent fine detail, consistent scale, proportion and perspective, believable light
  and shadow, smooth tonal gradations.") and uses negative "" (SCA: no suffix, negative " "). The suffix extends the
  Qwen-Image model card's own positive magic. Metrics score the benchmark prompt for both.

## Selection rule (fixed before any TUNE result)
- For each configuration: paired per-prompt differences against AReA at the same size on TUNE for PickScore,
  HPSv2.1 and ImageReward. Standardized effect d_z = mean difference / SD of the differences, per metric.
- Rank by the mean d_z over the three metrics. Within 0.10 of the best: the faster one wins.
- Guard rails (not ranking terms): MUSIQ_c and CLIP-IQA no worse than the untuned SCA by more than their own paired
  noise, and a visual check of all 12 TUNE images for duplication or broken composition.
- Single-pass configurations screen at 4096^2 against AReA 4096^2; progressive ones at 5312^2 (their stage factors are
  exact there: 1328 -> 2656 -> 5312) against AReA 5312^2 on TUNE. If the best of each family is within 0.10, the
  single-pass one also runs at 5312^2 on TUNE before the TEST run.
- TEST runs once, for the chosen one or two configurations, at 5312^2 on the 15 prompts; no further changes after.
