Baseline validation of the duplicate-subject detector (2026-11-02). Flags: scripts/dup_rate.py defaults (thr 0.35, sides 800,1333, nms 0.5, contain 0.9, contain-area 0.3, max-frac 0.9), any subject, identical to per_image_a4k195_any.csv.

Stratified validation sample of baseline images for the duplicate-subject detector.

Population: images of each method whose prompt has at least one subject with a numeric implied count
(the rows dup_rate.py writes with --per-image). Rule, one RNG = random.Random(20261102) used in the method
order area, hrdit, full, ultraimage: flagged and unflagged prompt ids are sorted; take
nf = min(#flagged, quota // 2) flagged ids with rng.sample, then quota - nf unflagged ids with rng.sample.
Afterwards the same RNG shuffles all sampled images once to assign blind ids v01..v50.

precision = TP/(TP+FP) in the flagged sample. recall_corrected = P*Nf / (P*Nf + (FN/n_unflagged)*Nu).
