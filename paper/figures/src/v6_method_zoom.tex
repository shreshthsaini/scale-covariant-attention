%       key at coarse position b; the query moves to c(p).
%   (b) Near view on the same grid (tile t = 2): two query tiles, their windows (w = n0 filled; w = 2 n0 dotted; the

%       block mask of the first tile (text keys grey, bridge dark amber, window amber).
%   (c) Gate and mix (sigma falls left to right): the weight of head 24 of block 12 (fig_method_zoom.py's example, chosen there for its large
%       r = 2 vs r = 4 gap; head 10 of Figure 3 is nearly flat in sigma) at r = 2 (dashed) and r = 4 (solid); dots:
%       the weight single-pass sampling applies at its 20 post-gate steps at 4096^2 (amber: the four wide-window
%       steps read between the tables; green: native steps at r = 4); grey: sigma > sigma_g, far view only. Below,
%       the mix itself.
%   (d) The r = 4 table: 57 blocks x 24 heads (sorted per block) x 14 noise bins, sigma rising upward.
