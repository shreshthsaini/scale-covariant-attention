Tuning SCA on Qwen-Image against AReA

SCA's Qwen-Image settings had been carried over from FLUX. 9 configurations were screened against AReA (its own defaults) on 12 TUNE prompts, and the selection rule fixed before any run chose Single pass with AReA's conditioning. 5 progressive configurations ranked higher on the preference metrics but failed the guard rail on native detail (soft 1024 px crops), so they were not eligible. The chosen configuration then ran once on the 15 held-out TEST prompts at 5312². Against AReA on TEST: PickScore -0.11 [-0.40, +0.17], HPSv2.1 -0.009 [-0.015, -0.002], ImageReward +0.03 [-0.09, +0.17], MUSIQ on 1024 px crops +5.4 [+4.2, +6.5]; against untuned SCA: PickScore +0.15 [-0.03, +0.34], HPSv2.1 +0.007 [-0.001, +0.015] (untuned SCA against AReA: PickScore -0.26 [-0.46, -0.08], HPSv2.1 -0.016 [-0.023, -0.010]). Median time per image: 621 s against AReA's 690 s and untuned SCA's 604 s (40 steps).
