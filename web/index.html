Abstract

Diffusion transformers trained near one megapixel can generate 4K images without retraining once their attention is adapted. Each such method must set how far every attention head looks, its reach, at a size the model never saw. Current methods share one schedule across heads or search a setting per size. We show that reach is a property of the head: at the training resolution of FLUX.1-dev, the head explains 97.5% of the variance of reach and the denoising step 2.5%. We introduce Scale-Covariant Attention (SCA), which mixes a native-size window and a native-size grid over the whole image with one number per head. Reach emulation fits that number once at 1024², where full attention still works, in closed form. Inside HRDiT’s sampler, SCA makes a 4096² image in 49.2 s, 4.1× faster than AReA, and beats AReA on all ten per-image metrics at three seeds. It is statistically indistinguishable from HRDiT on three preference scores in 46% of HRDiT’s time.

02The observation

Reach is a property of the head

Every training-free method must choose how far each head looks at a size the model never saw, where nothing can check the choice. At 1024², where full attention still works, we measured it for all 1,368 heads of FLUX.1-dev.

03The method

Two native-size views, one number per head

Each head mixes a near view and a far view, both at the trained size, with one weight: its reach.

1Two views

A native-size window and a native-size grid

The near view is a native-size window around the query, for edges and texture. The far view is a native-size grid over the whole image, for layout. Every offset in the window and the grid stays in the trained range, and the cost grows linearly with the pixel count.

Oi = πh Onear + (1 − πh) Ofar

2Reach emulation

Measured once at 1024²

Full attention fails at 4096², so nothing there can score a weight. Shrinking both views by the target’s ratio r rebuilds its proportions at 1024², where the model’s own full attention is the reference; the best weight has a closed form.

J(π) = E − 2πB + π2A,   π* = clip(B / A, 0, 1)

Where it falls short

  • Patch-level fidelityPatch FID is higher than HRDiT’s (+4.6 [0.6, 7.7]) and single-pass SCA’s (+9.2 [5.1, 13.2]); in its sampler, coarser weights have lower patch FID.
  • Hand-set partsThe opening, window sizes and bridge are set by hand, the window sizes on a 46-prompt subset of the evaluation set; only the weights are measured.
  • Larger sizesAt 6144² and 8192² the samples show block and stripe artifacts.
  • Single passIn single-pass mode, small additional figures occasionally appear in scenes with people or animals; the progressive configuration reduces this to the level of the multi-stage baselines.
